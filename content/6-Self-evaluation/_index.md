## Self-Assessment

During my internship in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program at **Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam** from **September 14, 2026** to **December 14, 2026**, I had the opportunity to translate my academic foundation in Information Assurance into practical cloud engineering and security workflows.

Throughout the program, I participated in hands-on cloud architecture labs, database migration exercises, serverless event-driven refactoring, and building a 12-week technical worklog website using Hugo and GitHub Pages. Through these activities, I significantly strengthened my skills in AWS cloud infrastructure design, Identity and Access Management (IAM), database migration (AWS DMS), serverless computing (Lambda, EventBridge, SQS), technical documentation, and cost optimization.

In terms of work ethic, I consistently strived to complete weekly milestones on schedule, adhered to program guidelines, and actively collaborated with mentors and peers to troubleshoot technical blockers and improve project outcomes.

### Areas for Improvement

- **Technical Research & Continuous Learning:** Develop a more structured habit of reading official AWS whitepapers, service documentation, and release updates to proactively keep pace with rapidly evolving cloud architectures and emerging technologies.
- **Time Management & Pacing:** Improve weekly schedule discipline and task estimation to ensure all lab modules and documentation are completed evenly without last-minute rush at the end of the program.
- **Architectural Troubleshooting & Problem-Solving:** Deepen root-cause analysis skills when handling complex cloud service configurations, permission boundaries, and cross-service integration issues.
- **Technical Presentation & Communication:** Enhance clarity and conciseness when presenting system architectures, documenting technical worklogs, and communicating progress in professional environments.
